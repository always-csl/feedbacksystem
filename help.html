Help & Support

Find helpful information about courses, assignments, feedback, and getting support.

Courses Registration

Need help registering for your courses? Follow the steps below.

1

Find Your Course

Navigate to your school's website and then go to the course page for the course you wish to register for.

2

Find the Registration Link

Find your course's registration link. This will likely be located in the syllabus or syllabus quiz provided by your instructor.

3

Register for the Course

Click the registration link or paste it into your browser's address bar. You may be prompted to log in to your account before registering for the course.

Submitting Assignments

Follow these steps to submit an assignment through Feedback System.

1

Open the Assignment List

Navigate to the Assignment List to see the assignments for each class you are enrolled in through the Feedback System.

2

Select Your Course

Select the course you would like to submit an assignment for using the course dropdown at the top of the page.

3

Upload Your Assignment

Select the submission option or click the assignment name. You will then find an upload assignment button where you can submit your assignment as a ZIP folder.

Feedback Results

Learn how Feedback System processes your assignment.

When you submit an assignment, it will be processed and compared to the correct code for that assignment.

If your code is correct, the system will display a success status for each step of the assignment. If your code does not match the expected code, the system will display errors for the assignment steps that were not completed correctly.

Check your file name

Your submission can also fail if you do not follow the appropriate file naming convention.

You can resubmit!

You can submit as many times as you like until you complete the assignment and receive success for all steps.
